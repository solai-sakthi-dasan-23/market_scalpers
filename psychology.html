The Three Killers

Why most traders in India fail.

⚡

FOMO

Fear of missing out causes impulsive entries with no system — chasing candles instead of waiting for a confirmed setup.

🔁

Revenge Trading

Placing bigger, angrier trades immediately after a loss to "win it back" — the single fastest way to blow an account.

📈

Overleveraging

Greed pushes traders to risk more than they can afford on a single position, turning one bad trade into a career-ending one.
