The Methodology

What is TBEM?

TBEM (Time-Based Entry Module) combines defined entry windows with structural confirmation — order blocks, fair value gaps, liquidity sweeps, rejections and news context — so every trade has an objective, repeatable trigger rather than a discretionary "feel."

Every trade referenced in these statistics was logged in real time, forward-tested across live Forex and Crypto markets — not curated after the fact.
