About Us

Market Scalpers is a forex, crypto and gold trading academy in Chennai, founded by Nykash in Triplicane. Since 2019 we've trained 1,600+ students through small classroom batches of up to 20 learners.

Our Story

From one trader's edge to a 1,600-student community.

Market Scalpers began with a simple frustration: most trading education in India sells signals and guaranteed returns instead of a repeatable process. Founder Nykash built the TBEM (Time-Based Entry Module) methodology from three-plus years of forward-tested trades across Forex, Crypto and Gold — then opened it up to working professionals across Tamil Nadu who wanted structure, not shortcuts.

Today, every batch is capped at 20 students so Nykash and the mentorship team can review real trades, not just teach theory. That's the whole model: small rooms, real accountability, lifetime access.
